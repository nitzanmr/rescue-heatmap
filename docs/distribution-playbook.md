# Distribution Playbook

The Venezuela retrospective highlighted a core distribution risk: a useful form that reaches the public late or through a narrow audience has little operational value.

## Guardrails

- Coordinate with incident authorities and trusted local organizations.
- Translate and locally review every message before release.
- Never promise rescue, identification, or a response time.
- Publish privacy, correction, deletion, and emergency-contact information.
- Measure reach without embedding personal information in analytics.

## Tier 1 — 0–6 hours

- Local WhatsApp and Telegram communities
- Local radio, television, and digital newsrooms
- Municipal and trusted civil-society channels
- Hospitals, shelters, and community leaders

## Tier 2 — 6–24 hours

- INSARAG coordination channels, when accepted by the relevant coordination structure
- OCHA and Red Cross/Red Crescent contacts
- National emergency-management and humanitarian partners

## Tier 3 — 24–72 hours

- X, Facebook, Reddit, and relevant diaspora communities
- Follow-up media coverage and correction posts
- Targeted outreach to geographic and demographic gaps

## Measurement

Track time to first trusted placement, submissions by coarse area, duplicate rate, moderation latency, language coverage, and referrals by channel. Do not optimize for raw submissions at the expense of safety or data quality.
