# Colombia Earthquake — August 2026

## Live-document notice

This is a developing-incident retrospective. USGS records an M7.4 earthquake near San José del Palmar, Colombia, on 10 August 2026. The requested casualty estimate of 70–80+ deaths was not verified at the time this document was drafted and must not be treated as an official figure.

## Current lesson

Six weeks between incidents was not enough to produce and operationally validate an MVP. The missing work was not only software: governance, privacy, moderation, distribution, local coordination, offline testing, and drills were also required.

## Immediate actions

- Preserve a timestamped source log and clearly label unverified claims.
- Do not deploy untested software into a live response.
- Capture requirements and gaps without collecting personal data.
- Turn each gap into an owner, deadline, and drill acceptance test.

## Review cadence

Update only from primary or reputable sources, record revisions, and separate verified facts from operational hypotheses.
