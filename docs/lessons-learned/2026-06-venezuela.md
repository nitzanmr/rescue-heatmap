# Venezuela Earthquakes — June 2026

## Verification status

USGS records include M7.2 and M7.5 earthquakes in Venezuela in June 2026. The requested figure of approximately 1,722 deaths has not been independently verified and is intentionally not presented here as fact. No public source verifying INSARAG adoption or endorsement of this project was found.

## Observations

- The reporting concept was introduced too late to build sustained local awareness.
- Distribution relied on a narrow audience rather than established local channels.
- A form alone is not an operational system; moderation, recipients, privacy, and follow-up must exist before promotion.

## Lessons

1. Keep a deployable, tested template before an incident.
2. Assign distribution and operational ownership at activation, not after launch.
3. Start with trusted local channels and languages.
4. Treat external interoperability or endorsement as unverified until documented in writing.
5. Measure whether reports become actionable assignments, not only submission volume.

## Follow-up

- Build and test an MVP.
- Create partner-reviewed translations and safeguarding procedures.
- Seek formal review from relevant disaster-response organizations.
